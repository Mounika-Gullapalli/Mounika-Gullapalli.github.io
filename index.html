U.S. Airline On-Time Performance Analysis

Statistical analysis of 604K+ U.S. flights (BTS dataset) examining whether delays vary meaningfully by airline and airport. Cleaned and validated the raw dataset down to a 1.22% rejection rate, then ran a one-way ANOVA (F = 275.44, p < 0.001) confirming significant delay differences across airlines, followed by an independent samples t-test with Cohen's d effect size and a 95% confidence interval to quantify the gap between the best- and worst-performing carriers. Also tested flight distance against arrival delay using Pearson correlation and identified the highest-delay airports.
